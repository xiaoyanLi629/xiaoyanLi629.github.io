Computer Vision2026

IACD: Iterative Adversarial Collaborative Detection

European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV) · Li, Jiang, Chen, Yang

A dual-perspective detection framework that pairs a frozen pretrained detector (the Seeker) with a lightweight trainable module (the Hider) that reasons inversely: given the environment, where would a target hide? The Hider identifies the Seeker's blind spots and residually amplifies neck features before a second detection pass, with no retraining of the base detector. Validated on illicit poppy cultivation and UAV imagery.

Brain × AI2026

The Temporal Efficiency Paradox: Fast Brains React, Slow Brains Understand

CogSci 2026 · corresponding author

An fMRI study of the cortical temporal hierarchy on about 65 hours of naturalistic movie data across seven functional networks. Slow, integrative networks encode content better than fast sensory ones, and sequence models (LSTM) outperform ridge regression uniformly across networks, showing that temporal integration is where the encoding advantage lives.

Apparent Inefficiency, Hidden Optimality

IEEE BIBM 2026 · corresponding author

Why does the brain's multimodal attention not track feature strength? Using encoding models over visual, auditory and language features from naturalistic movies, we show that cortical attention allocation dissociates from raw feature strength, and that this apparent inefficiency is consistent with an optimal integration strategy.

Attention Mechanisms for Theory of Mind

IEEE BIBM 2026 · regular paper

Transformers pass Theory-of-Mind behavioural tests, but do their internal representations align with the brain's ToM network? Comparing hidden states of GPT-2, Mistral and Qwen models with fMRI from 58 participants reading social versus physical narratives, we find only weak brain-transformer alignment despite behavioural success.

Brain × AI2026

Network Stability as the Hallmark of Neural Efficiency Under Cognitive Load

IEEE BIBM 2026 · short paper

Do neurally efficient people reorganize their brain networks when a task gets harder, or do they bring a better-organized network in the first place? Using N-back working-memory fMRI, we find that stability, not reorganization, is the signature of efficiency, and audit the result against leakage and threshold choices.

Routing-Level Signatures of Cognitive Efficiency

IEEE BIBM 2026 · regular paper

Multi-task HCP fMRI tested for routing-level signatures of cognitive efficiency. Only inter-subject routing consistency survives FDR correction, and a methodological caveat is made explicit: treating within-group pairwise correlations as independent inflates significance by many orders of magnitude, which a label-permutation null corrects.

Do Foundation Models Think Like Efficient Brains?

Tsinghua Science and Technology · under review

Mixture-of-Experts models route each input through a sparse subset of experts; efficient brains recruit sparser, more selective activation. This project compares sparse routing in Switch, Qwen and DeepSeek MoE models with task-fMRI activation sparsity to ask whether the two kinds of intelligence share a computational principle.

Orgalyst: Conversational Analysis of Organoid Bright-Field Images

AI4S Open Innovation · 5th Pazhou Algorithm Competition · AI + Organ-on-a-Chip, end-to-end system

A conversational analysis system for organoid bright-field microscopy. Every number is computed by a deterministic toolkit (segmentation, counting, morphology, growth and QC), and an assistant that knows the protocol plans the analysis, calls the tools and writes a reproducible report. Works across brain, colon, intestine, lung and PDAC organoids.

Analyst 2020⛶
Environmental AICV2020

Tap Water Fingerprinting with a CNN Built from Coffee-Ring Images

Analyst 145(4), 1511–1523 · Li, Sanderson, Allen, Lahr

A one-dollar, seconds-long water test: a droplet dried on aluminium leaves a coffee-ring residue that a phone camera can image, and a CNN classifies the pattern into water-chemistry groups with accuracy comparable to human clustering. The basis of a Transformer-U-Net follow-up that quantifies contaminant concentrations from 625 images and 4,200 EDS maps.
