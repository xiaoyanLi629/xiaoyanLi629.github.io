IACD: Iterative Adversarial Collaborative Detection via Dual-Perspective Blind Spot Discovery

Xiaoyan Li, Cuicui Jiang, Jiaoping Chen, Rumei Yang

European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026

codetheory (EART)

A frozen pretrained detector (the Seeker) is paired with a lightweight trainable Hider that reasons inversely: given the environment, where would a target hide? The Hider finds the Seeker's blind spots and residually amplifies neck features before a second pass, without retraining the base detector. Validated on illicit poppy cultivation and UAV imagery.

Network Stability as the Hallmark of Neural Efficiency Under Cognitive Load

Xiaoyan Li, Cuicui Jiang, Yujia Du, Jiaxuan Wei, Xingyue Liu, Jiaoping Chen*

IEEE BIBM, 2026 · short paper

code

With N-back working-memory fMRI (N = 200), both efficiency groups show nearly identical, minimal change in modularity from low to high load. Stability, not reorganisation, is the signature of efficiency, and a leakage audit shows why high "brain-based" classifier accuracies in this design cannot be trusted.

Routing-Level Signatures of Cognitive Efficiency: Only Inter-Subject Routing Consistency Survives FDR, with a Pairwise-Correlation Caveat

Xiaoyan Li, Cuicui Jiang, Jiaoping Chen, Yujia Du, Jiaxuan Wei, Xingyue Liu, Rumei Yang*

IEEE BIBM, 2026 · regular paper

code

Of four candidate routing signatures in multi-task HCP fMRI, only inter-subject routing consistency survives FDR (d = 0.52). Treating within-group pairwise correlations as independent overstates significance by about 28 orders of magnitude; a label-permutation null fixes it. Cortex and MoE expert routing share no detectable category structure.
